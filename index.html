From Muhammad, sincerely05 / 06
Not a perfect promise. An honest one.

My promise
to you, Sarauniya.

My Queen, 💜

I wish I could place my heart in your hands for a moment, just so you could see how deeply grateful I am that Allah allowed our paths to meet. You have brought so much goodness into my life, and loving you is something I never want to treat as ordinary.

I can't promise that the world will always be gentle with you. I can't promise that every day will be easy, or that I'll always find the perfect words. But I can promise that I want to keep showing up with sincerity. I want to listen, to speak honestly, to apologise when I'm wrong, to respect you when things are difficult, and to never treat your heart carelessly.

If people misunderstand you, I want to understand before I judge. If you're struggling, I want to make room for your truth. If we disagree, I want us to seek a way through it with respect—not turn each other into enemies.

And if I ever fall short, I hope I have the humility to recognise it and do better. My love for you should be visible not only in sweet words, but in my character, my choices, and the way I treat you.

I love you on the bright days, and I want to love you with patience on the difficult ones, too. Not because you must be perfect, and not because you owe me anything—but because you are precious to me.

May Allah protect your heart, ease what weighs on you, and guide us toward what is good. And if He grants us our Nikkah, insha'Allah, may He help me be a husband who brings you safety, respect, laughter, and sakinah.

Whatever the world says, please don't forget that you are more than its opinions. And whenever life feels heavy, I hope you remember there is someone who genuinely wants to hear you, understand you, and stand beside you—with Allah's guidance.

With all my love,

Your Muhammad. Your Hamzain. 🤍
